Healthcare platform

Medwell Pharmacy Patient Portal

A pharmacy workflow platform focused on patient medication visibility, interaction checks, and operational support for prescription handling.

  • Full Stack
  • Healthcare
  • Medication Tracking
  • Team Project

Problem

Pharmacy workflows can involve scattered patient information, manual checks, and repeated operational steps that slow down care delivery.

My Role

I contributed to the application design and development as part of a university team, with a focus on practical workflow support and usability.

Implementation

The portal concept included medication tracking, real-time interaction checks, and prescription-label printing to support safer pharmacy operations.

Outcome

The project demonstrated how healthcare software can reduce friction in daily pharmacy tasks while keeping patient needs visible.
